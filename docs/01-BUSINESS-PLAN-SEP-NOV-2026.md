## 3. What we are selling
- **Product**: flavoured instant coffee (agglomerated granules), 50 g glass jar, four flavours: Original, Belgian Chocolate, Hazelnut, Caramel. Launch with **two** (recommendation: Hazelnut + Original, or Hazelnut + Caramel — decide by supplier sample quality and 2023 sell-through; "hazelnut instant coffee" is the most searched flavour term on Amazon India).
- **Pack architecture** (all inside the ₹300–1,000 Amazon 0% referral band):
  - Single 50 g jar — MRP ₹349 (placeholder; finalise after quotes; comparable flavoured 50–60 g jars sit ₹299–449)
  - Duo pack (2 × 50 g) — ₹649 (gifting, Diwali)
  - Four-flavour box (4 × 50 g) — ₹999 (only when all four are in stock — January)
- **Positioning**: "Real instant coffee, made for how India drinks it" — with milk, cold, dalgona. Honest small brand, registered company since 2022, FSSAI licensed, from Pune; run by a creator people already watch.

## 6. Unit economics (placeholders until quotes; ex-GST)
Single jar MRP ₹349 → net of 5% GST ₹332.

| Line | Own site | Amazon FBA |
|---|---|---|
| Net revenue | 332 | 332 |
| Landed COGS (finished labelled jar, placeholder ₹120–160) | −140 | −140 |
| Referral fee | – | 0 (band ₹300–1,000; re-check at 7% = −23) |
| Closing fee | – | −26 (₹301–500 band, FBA; verify in Seller Central) |
| Shipping / FBA weight handling | −45 (Shiprocket 500 g) | −40 (est.) |
| Payment gateway | −5 (Cashfree UPI 0%; cards ~1.9%) | – |
| Outer packaging | −12 | −8 (FBA prep) |
| RTO / returns provision | −10 | −8 |
| **Contribution** | **≈ ₹120 (36%)** | **≈ ₹110 (33%)** |

Duo pack at ₹649 roughly doubles contribution per order because shipping is per order. Push the duo pack everywhere.

Break-even on fixed costs (~₹6,000/month: hosting ₹0–1,000, Shiprocket plan ₹0, email ₹0, CA ₹2,500, tools) ≈ 50 orders/month ≈ 2 a day.
