# SOPs and Protocols — index

The full text of every SOP lives in `ops/sops/*.md` and is rendered inside Buzzcaf Ops (→ Protocols). This page is the index and the reason each exists.

| SOP | Title | Trigger | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOP-01 | Supplier qualification and enquiry | Any new manufacturer | Supply failure ended the business in 2023; you carry the maker's liability as Relabeller |
| SOP-02 | Sample evaluation and 4-week shelf test | Before any production order | Clumping/stale complaints in 2023 were moisture; this catches it for ₹0 |
| SOP-03 | Batch receiving and QC (6 checks) | Every delivery | Recall trail, FSSAI annual return evidence, protects Amazon account health |
| SOP-04 | Label and Legal Metrology pre-press checklist | Before print, and on the printer's proof | A wrong label is a recall, not a reprint |
| SOP-05 | Complaint handling and recall trail | Every complaint | 3 on one batch = pattern; retention samples settle disputes |
| SOP-06 | FBA inbound shipment | Every shipment to Amazon | Shelf-life-remaining rules, Retailer KOB, receipting time |
| SOP-07 | Website order fulfilment | Every site order | Pack, Shiprocket, invoice, WhatsApp update, review nudge |
| SOP-08 | Monthly close | 1st working day | GST, reconciliation, Amazon reports, backups, CA hand-off |
| SOP-09 | Document control and renewals | Monthly dashboard check | Nothing expires unnoticed again (FSSAI lapsed for two years) |
| SOP-10 | Security and access | Onboarding/offboarding, quarterly | Live key was in plaintext; 2FA; password manager |
| SOP-11 | Content and product-placement workflow with the YouTube channels | Every upload | The channels are the acquisition engine; consistency is the whole trick |
| SOP-12 | Daily inventory reconciliation across Amazon and site | Daily while < 30 orders/day | Overselling on Amazon damages metrics |

Governance: each SOP has a version and "last reviewed" date in Ops. Review all twelve at the November close.
