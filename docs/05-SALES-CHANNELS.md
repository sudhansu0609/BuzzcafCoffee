## Pricing discipline
- Same MRP everywhere (Legal Metrology; also Amazon price-parity monitoring). Discounts via coupons on the site, via Amazon deals/coupons on Amazon.
- Stay within ₹300–1,000 per listing on Amazon. Duo ₹649 and four-box ₹999 are designed for this band.
